Now four clauses: (a) DECLINE — fell in `Consecutive cycles` consecutive cycles at any magnitude; (b) SHARP DROP — fell by ≥1.5 in a single cycle, a magnitude deliberately fixed because it is a property of the 10-point scale rather than of the project; (c) PERSISTENT LAGGARD — lowest for `Consecutive cycles` consecutive cycles AND not improving, so a category that is lowest but RECOVERING does not trigger and §6a must say so; (d) ABSOLUTE FLOOR — at or below `Policy threshold`, kept as a backstop. `Policy threshold` and `Consecutive cycles` keep their names and types, so the config schema, the project form and INV-16's three-copy parity are untouched.
